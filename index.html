Progression has a history.

Immensa is a fantasy world so vast the horizon seems flat, and so old that the ancients had ancients. Classes, Skills, tiers, magical materials and personal advancement have shaped civilizations for tens of thousands of years.

Most progression fantasy asks what happens when a person has a System. Immensa asks what happens when civilizations have had one for ages.

Guilds, roads, laws, religions, ecologies, professions, ruins, monsters and cities all carry the consequences.

Scale

The horizon seems flat.

Distance still matters. Roads can run for thousands of miles, continents remain consequential, and extraordinary shortcuts belong to the world rather than erasing it.

Deep time

The ancients had ancients.

There was no single golden age. Different eras mastered different things, misunderstood older ruins and left usable histories behind.

Lived consequence

The System changed civilization.

Progression belongs to professions, institutions, ecology, law, trade, worship and infrastructure—not only to adventurers and combat builds.

About the project

Immensa is being discovered as it is built.

Immensa Chronicles is an ongoing worldbuilding and fiction project by Yali Zak-Malkin. It is being developed toward a progression-fantasy / LitRPG serial, alongside stories, essays, maps and other ways of entering the world.

The setting is not presented as a finished encyclopedia. New histories, journeys, societies, creatures, places and connections continue to be developed, tested against one another and revised.

Creative direction

World direction, curation and decisions about what becomes part of Immensa are by Yali Zak-Malkin.

AI-assisted development

AI tools are used extensively for research, analysis, prototyping, drafting and software development. They are part of the process, not the identity of the project.
