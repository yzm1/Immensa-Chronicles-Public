# Immensa Chronicles

**The world keeps going.**

A one-way road can carry a dying child nine hundred miles in a day—and leave her family twenty-three years apart. Dragons write names across miles of evening sky. A stair descends past its thousandth landing to a black sea, ships, a city on the far shore, and more stairs below.

**Immensa** is an ongoing fantasy worldbuilding and fiction project by **Yali Zak-Malkin**. It is a world so vast the horizon seems flat, so old that the ancients had ancients, and so long shaped by Classes, Skills, tiers and advancement that progression itself has an archaeology.

## The world

Immensa is deliberately recognizable fantasy: guilds, parties, dragons, monsters, dungeons, magical materials, Classes, breakthroughs, legendary craftspeople, dangerous expeditions and spectacular power all belong here.

But none of them arrived yesterday.

Classes can go extinct. Skills can survive as dances and folk practices. Roads can outlive their destinations. Professions inherit titles whose original qualifications disappeared. Creatures keep migrations learned in vanished ecologies. Ancient civilizations excavated civilizations already ancient to them—and sometimes misunderstood what they found.

**Progression has a history.** Not only characters do. The world does.

Advancement also never discards the setting for a succession of higher realms. The entire ladder remains in one world. Becoming stronger can make old geography newly traversable, survivable or legible; it does not make the hometown, road, ocean or person you knew at the beginning cease to matter.

Immensa should repeatedly produce the feeling that the world was larger, older or stranger than you thought—and then, later, that you were still thinking too small.

## A lived world, not a setting diagram

The extraordinary has ordinary life around it. There are inns on famous descents, bakeries and repair shops beside impossible roads, children growing up, bad coffee, arguments, festivals, debts, animals, meals and people deciding whether to come home.

The project cares about what spectacular things do to ecology, law, trade and civilization, but consequence is not meant to domesticate wonder. A forest of impossible trees gets to be wondrous first. A dragon can simply be a dragon. A world wonder can have weeds.

## Still being built

Immensa is not a finished encyclopedia. Hundreds of short stories, essays and interlocking world records are being used to explore the setting from different people, places, scales and eras.

Some pieces become foundations. Some are revised, contradicted or replaced. Places recur and acquire history. Some blank spaces are work not done yet; others are genuinely unknown inside the world. The project is allowed to keep both kinds of horizon.

## Where this is going

Immensa is being developed toward a long-form **progression-fantasy / LitRPG serial**. The existing stories and worldbuilding are not merely preparatory notes: they are ways of finding the places, histories, creatures, relationships and recurring pleasures strong enough to support that story before its opening is locked.

The eventual serial should begin with one person in one small part of the world. The promise is that there is genuinely more world beyond every horizon—and that progression reveals more of it rather than replacing it.

## Development process

AI tools are used extensively for research, corpus analysis, prototyping, drafting and software development. Creative direction, selection, revision, curation and final decisions about Immensa are by **Yali Zak-Malkin**. AI is part of how the project is being built; it is not what the project is about.
